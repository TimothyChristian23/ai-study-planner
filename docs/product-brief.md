# Product Brief

## Problem

Students often have course information spread across syllabi, PDFs, slide decks, notes, assignments, and learning management systems. The work of turning that pile into an actionable study plan is manual, repetitive, and easy to postpone.

## Solution

AI Study Planner ingests course materials and important dates, then produces an adaptive study workflow. It recommends what to study, creates practice questions, tracks weak topics, and answers questions using the student's own documents as the source of truth.

## Primary Users

- College students managing multiple courses
- Students preparing for exams from large sets of notes
- Learners who want active recall instead of passive rereading

## Core Jobs

- Upload course materials quickly
- Extract key topics, deadlines, and exam dates
- Generate a weekly study schedule
- Practice with quizzes based on actual materials
- See weak topics and review priorities
- Ask questions and get cited answers from uploaded documents

## Differentiators

- Course-material grounded answers
- Weak-topic feedback loop
- Schedule generation from real deadlines
- Portfolio-friendly demonstration of AI, retrieval, planning, and progress tracking
